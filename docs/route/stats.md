## Notes

- Ford position: the OSM ford node (4417047980) sits at km 6.64 on this line (and at km 6.65 on the raw OSM join), not km 5.84 as the project file says; the DEM dip (~674 m) confirms it. The 5.84 figure looks like a measurement slip in the earlier pass.
- The minimum radius is set by a kink limiter in build_route.py (R >= 10 m): the OSM line is coarse and its node corners (the Robinsons/Cattlemens junction, the ford V) would otherwise give radii of 2-5 m.
- Elevation: DEM is photogrammetric (stereo imagery); under dense canopy it is smoother/less reliable. Smoothed road profile = 9 m median + Gaussian sigma 15 m (5 m near the ford).

Bush Drift

Drift a LandCruiser 79 down 7.8 km of real dirt road, from Alexanders Hut in the South East Forest (NSW) to the Tantawangalo Creek bridge. Hit a tree, a log or a post and the run's over.

▶ Play in your browser ⬇ Android app Tell me how it went Browser: a computer with a keyboard is best (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari; about 12 MB). On an Android phone, get the app (5 MB): steps below.

Click the game once first so it gets the keyboard and the sound.

Scoring

  • Slide and you fill the pot: the more sideways and the faster, the more points.
  • Keep sliding and the multiplier climbs (up to ×9).
  • Drive straight for a couple of seconds, or press B, to bank pot × multiplier.
  • Crash and the pot is lost. Ditches slow you; trees, logs and posts end the run.

Android: installing the app

  1. On your phone, tap ⬇ Android app above. It downloads bush-drift.apk.
  2. Open the download. Android asks to allow installing apps from your browser: tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, then go back.
  3. Tap Install. If Play Protect warns about an unknown developer, choose More details → Install anyway (it's not from the Play Store, that's all).
  4. Turn the phone sideways. Steer with the two buttons on the left; GAS, BRAKE and HAND (handbrake) on the right; pause and restart at the top.

Android 10 or newer. iPhones: not yet (try the browser version on an iPad or computer).

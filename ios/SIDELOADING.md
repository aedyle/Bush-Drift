# Bush Drift on your iPhone (play test)

Bush Drift is a small drifting game on a real dirt road in the NSW bush. This is a test version, so it isn't on the
App Store. You install it yourself from a computer, using your own Apple ID. It's free, and it takes about 10 minutes.

**What you need:** the file `BushDrift.ipa`, a Windows PC or a Mac, a cable for your iPhone, and your Apple ID.

## 1. Install Sideloadly on your computer
Download it from **sideloadly.io** and install it. On Windows, follow its setup notes. It needs Apple's iTunes and
iCloud installed from Apple's website, not the Microsoft Store versions.

## 2. Install the game
1. Plug the iPhone into the computer. If the phone asks **Trust This Computer?**, tap Trust and enter your passcode.
2. Open Sideloadly. Your iPhone should appear at the top.
3. Drag `BushDrift.ipa` onto Sideloadly.
4. Type your Apple ID email and press **Start**. Enter your Apple ID password, and the code Apple sends you if it asks.
   Sideloadly uses your Apple ID only to sign the app for your own phone. If you'd rather not use your main Apple
   ID, a spare free one works too.
5. Wait for **Done**.

## 3. Let the phone open it (first time only)
1. **Developer Mode:** Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode → on. The phone restarts; when it's back,
   tap **Turn On** and enter your passcode.
2. **Trust the app:** Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → tap your Apple ID → **Trust**.
3. Open **Bush Drift**.

## Playing
- Hold the phone sideways. Steer with the arrows on the left. GAS, BRAKE and HAND(brake) are on the right. Pause is
  top left and restart (R) is top right.
- Drive from Alexanders Hut to the Tantawangalo Creek bridge. A tree or a post ends the run, and the ditch slows you.
- A Bluetooth game controller works too.

## Please send back
- A **screenshot while driving**. The small numbers in the top-left corner (fps, worst, slow) show how smoothly it
  runs on your phone.
- Does it get warm? Does it stutter anywhere? Is the sound OK? Do the buttons feel right?
- Anything else: https://aedyle.github.io/Bush-Drift/ has a feedback form.

## After 7 days
With a free Apple ID, apps you install yourself stop opening after 7 days. To keep playing, plug the phone in and do
step 2 again with the same file. Your records are kept.
